## Criteria

1. 3-8 objectives, each measurable (observable verb, no "understand/know/learn"), each with a Bloom level.
2. At least half at Apply or above.
3. Every objective maps to a needs-analysis task.
4. Depth targets name specific rules, sources, doctrines or data.
5. Every real-law claim in the content map names a primary source.
6. Assessment plan meets QAS minimums for projected credits (Std 5.01.2.1, 6.01.2). Exam covers at least 75% of objectives.
7. No invented model-policy limits. Every number must be in the Model Credit Policy or the Formula Library.

**Key.** Pass = met as written. Fixed = defect corrected in the blueprint during this review. Fail = still not met.

## Observations (not Gate 1 failures)

1. **Question-counting rule.** C0-C2 do not count scored practice in their credits; C3-C14 do (coverage review open item 7). Settle this before the credit worksheets.
2. **LD-02 and objective counts.** Thirteen blueprints exceed the Course Bible's level 2-3 aim, and several exceed 3-6 objectives. QAS (3-8) is met. The owner must amend the Bible.
3. **Two-verb objectives.** C9 O7, C11 O1, C12 O1 and O7 pair two verbs. That is measurable, but it breaks LD-01's one-verb rule. Tighten at lesson build.
4. **Named but unopened sources.** Many `[VERIFY: not opened]` cites meet criterion 5 because they name a primary source. Each must be opened before its chunk is written.
5. **Rate consistency.** C10 P4 and P5 price Cedar Court at 7.65%/12.65% with no month stated. Under D-CORE-27, any date from month 10 uses 9.00%. State the month.
