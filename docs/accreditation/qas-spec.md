# Accreditation build spec: NASBA QAS Self Study

Model policy for training - not for live lending decisions.

Researched 22 Nov 2026. Primary source: *Statement on Standards for CPE Programs*, AICPA/NASBA, **August 2026 edition** ("Std"), [PDF](https://cdn.asp.events/CLIENT_NASBA_287596D2_5056_B733_49DFF69B632BDF66/sites/nasba-registry-composer-2/media/Documents/2026-Statement-on-Standards-for-CPE-Programs.pdf). It applies to new QAS applications from 1 Aug 2026 and to programs first published from 1 Mar 2027. Secondary target: ANSI/IACET 1-2018 (CASE v1.2, revised Aug 2025).

## Requirements per module (MUST)

| # | Requirement | Rule | Std ref |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Learning objectives | Measurable verbs; 3-8 per module; knowledge level stated | 3.01, 3.01.1 |
| 2 | Prerequisites and advance preparation | Precise for Intermediate; "none" where not applicable | 3.02.1 |
| 3 | Dates | Publication or last-review date shown | 4.01 |
| 4 | Development | Subject matter expert develops; AI-assisted content checked for accuracy by the developer | 4.01.1 |
| 5 | Independent review | Independent content reviewer before first release and after significant revision | 4.02 |
| 6 | Self-study materials | Overview of topics, search or index, glossary, navigation and completion instructions, review questions with feedback, final exam | 4.05.3 |
| 7 | Not reading-plus-test | Interactive elements required | 4.05.3 |
| 8 | Review questions | At least 3 scored items per credit, placed throughout; fractions: 0.2->0, 0.4->1, 0.5->2, 0.6->2, 0.8->3; true/false does not count; no pass rate | 5.01.2.1 |
| 9 | Review feedback | At least "correct" or "incorrect"; we also explain every option | 5.01.2.2 |
| 10 | Final exam | At least 5 items per credit (fractions 0.2->2, 0.4->3, 0.5->4, 0.6->4, 0.8->5); no true/false or yes/no; no repeats of review questions; covers at least 75% of objectives; pass mark at least 70% before credit | 6.01.2 |
| 11 | Exam feedback | Optional; without a test bank, no feedback on a failed attempt; with a bank, large enough to limit overlap on retakes | 6.01.2 |
| 12 | Completion | Self-certification alone is not enough; the exam decides | 6.01 |
| 13 | Credit | 1 credit = 50 minutes; self-study in 0.2 or 0.5 increments; round down | 7.01 |
| 14 | Credit method | Word-count formula (or pilot test with at least 3 independent testers) | 7.02 |

**Word-count formula (Std 7.02.5-.7):** credits = [(words ÷ 180) + audio/video minutes + (all questions × 1.85)] ÷ 50. "All questions" counts review questions, exercises and exam items. Exclude introduction, instructions, bios, table of contents, glossary, pre-test and appendixes. Video counts only if it is not narration of the text.

**Worked example:** 8,000 words, 12 review questions, 24 exam items, 6-minute video. 8,000 ÷ 180 = 44.444; (12 + 24) × 1.85 = 66.600; + 6.000 = 117.044 minutes ÷ 50 = 2.341, rounded down to 2.2 credits (fifths). Minimums at 2.2 credits: review 3 × 2 + 0 = 6; exam 5 × 2 + 2 = 12.

## Our build rule for item counts
Size each module after its lesson is final: compute credits, then write at least the minimums plus a bank. Target: review questions = 4 per credit (rounded up); exam bank = 2 parallel forms (Set A, Set B), each with at least 6 items per credit and at least 2 items per objective, no overlap with review questions. Multiple choice only, four options, one best answer, feedback for every option.

## Course-level requirements

| Item | Rule | Ref |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evaluations | Collected for every program: objectives met; prerequisites appropriate; materials and exam relevant; time allotted appropriate; author effective; open comments. Results reviewed periodically | Std 4.04.1-.2 |
| Advance disclosure | Objectives, program type (QAS Self Study), credits and field, prerequisites, knowledge level, advance preparation, description, registration, refund and cancellation, complaint policy, NASBA sponsor statement | Std 8.01, 8.01.1 |
| Certificate | Sponsor name; participant; course title; completion date; location if any; program type; credits by field; NASBA sponsor ID; state registration number if required; 50-minute-hour statement; board statements; IACET CEUs if applicable | Std 9.01; IACET 8.3 |
| Records | Per learner: completion, credits, date; plus credit worksheets, author and reviewer credentials, evaluation results, marketing and course materials; 7 years | Std 9.02; IACET 8.4-8.6 |
| Expiration | Course expiration at most 1 year from enrollment (integrated series may run longer) | Std 9.02.2(3) |
| IACET extras | Needs analysis linked to outcomes; identity check; learner told whether criteria met; records privacy policy; anti-discrimination and proprietary-interest disclosures; 1 CEU = 10 contact hours | IACET 1.6, 1.7, 4.1, 5.2, 8.1-8.6 |

## Application (later, owner action)
QAS application with one full sample program, policies, author bios, sample evaluation and certificate, grading policy and the measurement worksheet ([NASBA QAS self-study](https://www.nasbaregistry.org/preparing-to-apply/qas-self-study)). Fees are tiered by total credits and renewed yearly.

## Not confirmed
QAS eligibility for internal training with non-CPA audiences (ask cpe@nasba.org); IACET self-paced contact-hour method (guidance PDF unavailable); some state boards limit fractional credit. NMLS CE does not apply (business-purpose loans fall outside 12 U.S.C. 5102(9)).
